## Minor release — hand-drawn Acorn artwork

A texture update only. Gameplay, balance and recipes are identical to 1.1.1.

### Changed

* **The Acorn now uses hand-drawn pixel art.**
  The previous texture was downscaled from a 1254x1254 illustration, which left it soft-edged with
  100 blended colours. It is replaced by a purpose-drawn **16x16, 39-colour** sprite with hard edges
  and no compression muddiness. Content is 9x10 within the canvas.

* **Acorn Jam now uses a 32x32 texture.**
  That artwork comes from the same illustration, and at 16x16 the label detail was crushed. At 32x32
  the acorn motif on the label, the glass highlights and the lid texture are all legible.
  (624 colours - it is a downscaled illustration rather than pixel art.)

* The two items therefore use different canvas sizes (16x16 and 32x32). Minecraft supports both; a
  hand-drawn 16x16 Acorn Jam is planned so the set can be unified later.

* The mod icon was updated to match.

Both items appear slightly smaller than in 1.1.1, since the new Acorn artwork has more transparent
margin around it.
